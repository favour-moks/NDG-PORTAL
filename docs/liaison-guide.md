# State Liaison Guide

*A one-page guide to checking your state's status in the NDG Payment Accreditation Portal. You'll use this twice a year, not every day — nothing here assumes you remember the last time.*

## 1. Sign in

You'll receive an email invite from the Secretariat with a link. Open it on your phone or computer, choose a password, and you're in — no password is ever sent to you by email; you always set your own.

Next time, go to the portal's sign-in page, enter your email and password.

## 2. You'll land on your state automatically

There is no menu to search or state to pick — as soon as you sign in and select "Participants," you see your own state's list. You cannot see any other state, by design.

If you see "No state has been assigned to your account yet," contact an admin — your account needs a state assigned before you can see anything.

## 3. Choose an edition

If more than one edition (Games year) exists, a dropdown near the top lets you switch between them. Your state's history is available across every past edition, not just the current one.

## 4. Filter to find someone

Use the search box to type a name, or the dropdowns to narrow by category, sport, or bank. The list updates as you filter — no separate "search" button to press.

## 5. Check status

Each row shows:
- **Arrival accredited** — whether that person has been checked in.
- **Payment status** — Paid, In Progress, or Pending.

You will **never** see an amount of money anywhere in the portal — not in the list, not in a downloaded file, not in a person's detail view. That's deliberate: your role is to answer "were they paid," not to see how much anyone was paid.

Click "View" on any row to see that person's full history across every edition, in the same state-only, no-money view.

## 6. Download a copy

"Export Excel" or "Export PDF" at the bottom of the list downloads exactly what's on your screen — same filters, same rows, same columns (still no money figures). Useful for sharing a status list with your delegation.

## If something looks wrong

- **"Access denied"** — you followed a link to a page you don't have permission for. This is normal if you clicked an old link meant for an editor; go back to Participants.
- **A list that used to show data is now empty** — your state assignment may have changed. Contact an admin.
- **You can't sign in at all** — your account may have been deactivated, or your invite link expired. Contact an admin for a fresh invite.

Every time you sign in and every list you view is logged — this is how the Secretariat knows the portal is actually being used, and how a viewing mistake (someone reaching your state's data who shouldn't) would be caught.
